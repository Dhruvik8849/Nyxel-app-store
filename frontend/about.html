About Nyxel

Nyxel is a product-grade independent app distribution platform built to help developers publish and users discover Android applications outside the major stores. We combine clear app metadata, transparent release notes, and developer pages with an emphasis on safety and user control. Our goal is to lower the friction for app creators while providing users with the information they need to make confident install decisions.

What We Do

App distribution

Publish APKs with version history, banners, and screenshots.

Developer support

Tools for managing releases, change logs, and developer profiles.

Secure APK hosting

Managed hosting with signature checks and scan indicators (mock in demo).

What We Have Done

  • Serving thousands of users
  • Hosting hundreds of apps
  • Trusted by independent developers

Mission

Empower developers with a lightweight, transparent distribution channel and give users clear context to evaluate and install apps safely.

Vision

A healthier app ecosystem where discovery and trust are first-class citizens — independent, open, and community-driven.
