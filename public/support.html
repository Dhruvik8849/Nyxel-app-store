FAQs

How do I publish an app?

Create a developer account, upload your APK, provide screenshots and release notes. The demo does not include a publisher UI.

Is Nyxel secure?

We provide signature checks and mock scans. For production, enable HTTPS and server-side scanning.

How do I report an issue?

Use the contact form or send an email to hello@nyxel.example with details and reproduction steps.
